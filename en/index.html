FAQ

Common questions before calling.

Where do you work?

Everything is delivered remotely, so I work with businesses wherever they are. Calls, support and documentation are in English.

Do you help individuals or only businesses?

My work is designed for businesses, freelancers and small companies: equipment, email, website, tools and business processes.

How much does it cost to have an IT person for my business?

There are two ways of working: monthly plans from €39/month + VAT (IT, website or operations & AI) or projects with a fixed quote before we start. The first call is free.

Can you fix problems remotely?

Yes. With AnyDesk I connect to your computer with your permission, and most software, email, account and website issues are solved on the spot. If something turns out to be physical, such as hardware or cabling, I'll tell you exactly what is needed.

Can you help with GDPR and NIS2 compliance?

Yes. I review the company's situation, prioritize the technical and organizational measures, and support you with the documentation and implementation. This also applies if you are a supplier to a company that requires you to comply with NIS2.

What's the difference between IT support and the digitalization assessment?

Support fixes specific technical issues. The assessment looks at how the whole company works (processes, tools and dependence on the owner) to decide what to organize, automate or change.
