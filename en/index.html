FAQ

Common questions before calling.

Where do you work, and what hours?

Everything is delivered remotely, so I work with businesses and agencies wherever they are. I am available for calls from 9:00 to 13:00 Eastern Time (6:00 to 10:00 Pacific). Updates and documentation are in English and are written so you can read them asynchronously.

How much does it cost?

Every project has a fixed quote before we start, in US dollars and excluding taxes. An operations audit starts at $750 and an Automation Sprint (one process automated end to end) starts at $3,000. The first call is free.

How do you get paid?

50% when we start and 50% on delivery, by bank transfer, Wise or card, in US dollars.

Who owns the code and the accounts?

You do. Code, automations and accounts are in your name, and everything is documented. Ownership passes to you when the final payment is made.

What happens if something breaks after delivery?

Integration and custom-tool projects include 30 days of adjustments after delivery. After that you can book ongoing support or ask for a one-off fix, so nothing depends on guessing who built it.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. If a client asks for something technical that your team does not build (an integration, an automation, a custom tool), I can build it under your brand at a fixed price.

Do you help individuals or only businesses?

My work is designed for businesses, freelancers and small companies: tools, data and business processes.
