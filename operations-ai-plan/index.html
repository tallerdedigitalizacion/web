When it makes sense

  • You have automations or integrations running and nobody watches them: when they fail, you hear about it from a customer.
  • The AI agent answers with outdated information because nobody has told it about the changes.
  • Process improvements stay half-done because day-to-day work eats the time.
  • An internal tool depends on the only person who knows how it works.
  • You solve problems one by one, but they come back somewhere else in the chain.
The approach

Operations are a chain, not a list of fixes

Most companies rebuild their operations every time something changes: a new customer, a new tool, a new person. The problem isn't in each step on its own, but in the fact that nobody designs or looks after the whole chain.

This plan is the continuation of that design: an external technical lead who knows your operations, maintains what already works and proposes the next step every month, instead of reacting when something breaks.
