Signs your company needs maintenance

  • Every month a different emergency shows up, always at the worst moment.
  • Nobody knows for sure whether the backups work.
  • Computers run different versions of Windows, Office or antivirus.
  • When someone new joins, setting up their computer and access takes days.
  • When someone leaves, they still have access to email or shared folders.
  • The router, wifi or server still has the factory password.

      FAQ

      Common questions

      What's the difference between maintenance and one-off support?

      One-off support fixes what has broken. Maintenance checks your equipment regularly so fewer things break and, when something does, it's all documented and backed up.

      How much does IT maintenance cost for a small business?

      The plan starts at $49/month, excluding taxes. The final fee depends on the number of computers, servers and services to cover; after the initial review I give you a fixed quote.

      Is there a long-term commitment?

      No long lock-in periods. If the service isn't useful to you, you can leave.

      Do you need to come to the office?

      No. The plan is delivered remotely. If a problem turns out to be physical (a failed disk, cabling), I'll tell you exactly what is needed.
