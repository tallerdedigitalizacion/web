Cookie policy

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that are downloaded to the user's device when accessing certain web pages.

Cookies make it possible, among other things, for a website to work properly, remember certain preferences, obtain statistical information about browsing or make integration with third-party services easier.

Similar technologies may also be used, such as pixels, tags, browser local storage, identifiers or third-party scripts. In this policy, the term “cookies” is used broadly to include these technologies where applicable.

3. What types of cookies does this website use?

This website may use first-party and third-party cookies for different purposes.

3.1. Technical or necessary cookies

These are the cookies needed for the website to work properly and to provide the services requested by the user.

They may be used, for example, to:

  • allow browsing the website
  • remember technical preferences
  • keep the site secure
  • manage forms
  • remember basic settings
  • allow interactive elements to work

These cookies do not require consent when they are strictly necessary to provide a service requested by the user or for the basic operation of the site.

3.2. Analytics or measurement cookies

These are cookies that provide information about how users use the website, which pages they visit, how long they stay, where they come from or what actions they take.

This website may use Google Analytics 4 to analyse the use of the website and to improve its content, navigation, performance and calls to action.

The information collected may include technical data such as IP address, browser, device, pages visited, interaction events, date, time and source of the visit.

Google tags (Google Analytics 4 and Google Ads measurement) are loaded on every visit, but with consent denied by default (Consent Mode v2). Until the user accepts the corresponding category, no Google cookie is written and Google only receives technical signals without cookies, such as the page visited, the source of the visit (for example, campaign parameters) and browser data, in addition to the IP address of any connection. Those signals are not linked to a persistent browser identifier and are used to measure in aggregate and to estimate conversions. If the user accepts analytics, Google Analytics starts using cookies; if the user accepts marketing, Google Ads does too.

3.3. Marketing or advertising cookies

These are cookies that make it possible to measure the results of the ads that Taller de Digitalización publishes on Google and to attribute to each ad the enquiries it generates.

For Google ads, conversion measurement may store the ad click identifier in the _gcl_aw cookie. That cookie is only written if the user accepts the "Marketing" category in the cookie panel.

3.4. Third-party cookies

This website may integrate or link to third-party services that may install cookies or similar technologies when the user interacts with them.

These services may include:

  • Google Analytics and Google Ads, for web analytics and ad measurement.
  • Cal.com, for call bookings.
  • Chatbase, for the chatbot or automated assistant.
  • WhatsApp, to make direct contact easier.
  • Technical services linked to custom forms or the site's own backend.

Each of these services may use its own cookies and process data under its own policies.

9. Consent

Necessary technical cookies may be used without prior consent, as they are needed for the basic operation of the website.

Analytics, advertising or third-party cookies that are not strictly necessary generally require the user's prior consent under the applicable regulations.

On the first visit, the website shows a banner that lets you reject, configure by category (analytics, help chat and marketing) or accept all non-necessary cookies. Rejecting is as easy as accepting.

Google Analytics cookies, Google Ads cookies, and Chatbase are not activated until the user gives consent for the corresponding category. Google tags are always loaded, with consent denied by default, and without consent they only send technical signals without cookies (section 3.2).
