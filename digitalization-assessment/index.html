FAQ

Common questions before the call.

Is this the same as an operational chaos diagnosis?

No. That one looks at processes and how much the business depends on the owner. This one focuses on your digitalization: tools, information flow, automations and integrations between platforms.

Do I have to change everything at once?

No. Recommendations are prioritized by impact and effort, so you can implement them in phases.

Are you going to sell me new tools?

No. The goal is not to sell you software, but to help you make better technology decisions.

How much does it cost?

Scope and price are adjusted to the size and complexity of the business. No commitment until you decide to move forward.
