      FAQ

      Common questions before getting started

      How does the money-back guarantee work?

      You have 30 days from the delivery of the first solution to try it. If you are not happy within that period, send an email to Taller de Digitalización asking for a refund. I refund in full what you paid for the first implementation, VAT included and through the same payment method, with the corresponding credit note and within one week. In return, you stop using what was delivered and do not keep the code or the solution. No excuses and no paperwork. The diagnosis is not refunded.

      Do the prices include VAT? How do I pay?

      Prices do not include VAT. Payment is in advance, each phase before it starts.

      How long does the first implementation take?

      I give you the timeline when the diagnosis is finished, once I know which problem gets solved and how.

      Is it safe to use AI with my customers' data?

      Your data is stored somewhere you control, such as a cloud account in your company's name, and I only work with what the process we automate needs. We agree that before we start. If you don't want information to go through an external AI model, a local model can be set up on your own server: that is a separate quote, but it can be done.

      I already have ChatGPT or Claude. Isn't that enough?

      They are good tools, but they answer when you ask them something. What I do is make the work happen on its own: I connect AI to your programs and your data, test it with your real cases and leave a person to validate what matters. A subscription doesn't do that for you.

      Will I have to hand control of my business to an AI?

      No. The AI prepares, cross-checks, alerts and proposes. Anything that costs money or can't be undone is always confirmed by a person.

      Are you a coach or a technician?

      A technician. I don't tell you how to run your business: you know that. I organise what already works and automate it.

      When is automating not worth it?

      When the process changes every week or only happens a couple of times a year. If that's your case, I'll tell you on the call and I won't sell you anything.

      Who owns the code?

      You do. The code, the accounts and the documentation are in your name, and ownership passes to you when the final payment is made.

      Do I have to replace all my current tools?

      Not necessarily. I look at what you already have first and only build what is needed, connecting it to what already works.

      Do you work in person or remotely?

      In person in Madrid and the surrounding area, and remotely for the rest of Spain. If a one-off visit is needed outside that area, we talk it through.

      Which language do we work in?

      English or Spanish, whichever you prefer: the call, the proposal and the documentation are in the language you choose.

      Do you use AI to write the code?

      Yes, to build faster. The judgement, the review of the work and the responsibility for the result are mine: I answer for what I deliver.
