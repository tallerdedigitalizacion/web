2. Purpose of this policy

This Privacy Policy explains clearly how the personal data of users who visit the website, contact the owner, book a call, fill in forms, complete self-assessments, download materials or interact with external tools embedded in the website is collected, used, stored and protected.

3. Personal data that may be processed

Depending on how the user uses the website, the following categories of data may be processed:

  • Identification data: first name, last name, job title or company.
  • Contact data: email, phone, WhatsApp or other means provided by the user.
  • Professional data: company name, sector, approximate size, operational situation, needs or comments voluntarily submitted.
  • Data entered in forms or self-assessments: answers, scores, observations and generated results.
  • Technical data: IP address, cookie identifiers, browser, device, pages visited, date and time of access, origin of the visit and browsing behaviour, where applicable.
  • Data derived from communications: messages sent through forms, email, WhatsApp, Cal.com, Chatbase or other enabled channels.

Specially protected information, such as health, ideology, religion, trade union membership, sexual orientation, biometric data or similar, is not intentionally requested. Users must refrain from providing this type of information through the forms, the chatbot or the contact channels.

4. Purposes of the processing

Personal data may be processed for the following purposes:

4.1. Answering enquiries and contact requests

The data necessary to answer messages sent through forms, email, WhatsApp, Cal.com, Chatbase or other enabled channels will be processed.

4.2. Managing call bookings

If the user books a free 15-minute call through Cal.com, the data necessary to manage the request, confirm the appointment, send reminders and keep communication related to that meeting will be processed.

4.3. Running self-assessments and generating results

If the user completes the Operational Tax self-assessment or similar tools, the answers entered will be processed to calculate scores, show results, generate an initial interpretation and, where applicable, allow the download of a diagnostic or report.

The self-assessment is indicative and does not replace a full professional diagnosis.

4.4. Managing custom forms

The forms on the website are custom-built and may send the information entered to a backend owned or controlled by the controller.

Data sent through these forms will be used only for the purpose stated in each case: contact, download of materials, self-assessment, information request, call booking or consented commercial follow-up.

4.5. Sending downloadable materials

If the user asks to download the method, reports, guides, documents or similar materials, the data provided will be processed to deliver the requested resource and to record the request.

4.6. Sending commercial or informational communications

No external email marketing tool is currently used.

Commercial communications, newsletters or informational content will only be sent where there is a legal basis, such as the user's prior consent or a previous contractual relationship that allows it.

Users may unsubscribe or withdraw their consent at any time by writing to info@tallerdedigitalizacion.com.

4.7. Analysing the use of the website

Technical and browsing data may be processed through Google Analytics, provided the user has given consent where required.

This data helps to understand how the website is used, improve content, detect technical problems and measure the effectiveness of campaigns, forms or calls to action.

4.8. Providing assistance through a chatbot

The website uses Chatbase as a chatbot or automated assistant. This tool lets the user ask questions about the services, methodology, prices, how the website works or other available content.

The messages entered by the user in the chatbot may be processed to generate answers and keep the tool running.

Users must not enter confidential, sensitive or unnecessary information in the chatbot.

4.9. Maintaining the security and operation of the website

Technical data strictly necessary to guarantee security and availability, prevent abuse, detect errors and ensure the correct operation of the website may be processed.

5. Legal basis for the processing

The legal bases for processing data are:

  • User consent: for forms, downloads of materials, subscription to communications, analytics cookies, use of tools that are not strictly necessary, or acceptance of specific purposes.
  • Pre-contractual measures: when the user asks for information, a call, a diagnosis or a service proposal.
  • Performance of a contract: when services are contracted.
  • Legitimate interest: to maintain the security of the website, prevent abuse, answer communications initiated by the user or keep basic evidence of the relationship.
  • Compliance with legal obligations: when certain information must be kept for tax, accounting, legal or administrative reasons.

6. Data retention

Personal data will be kept for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected.

In general:

  • Contact data will be kept while there is a relationship, an open request or a legitimate interest in keeping in touch.
  • Enquiry data will be kept for the time needed to answer and manage the request.
  • Data related to contracted services will be kept during the contractual relationship and afterwards for the applicable legal periods.
  • Data associated with commercial communications will be kept until the user withdraws consent or asks to unsubscribe.
  • Self-assessment data may be kept as long as needed to generate the result, allow follow-up or attend the user's request.
  • Technical data and cookies will be kept as stated in the Cookie Policy.
  • Conversations held through Chatbase may be kept according to the service configuration and its applicable policy.

Once the applicable periods have ended, data will be deleted or blocked where appropriate.

7. Recipients and processors

Personal data will not be sold to third parties.

However, to provide the services and keep the website running, external providers may process data on behalf of the controller, such as:

  • Hosting and web service providers.
  • Own backend or technical provider used to process custom forms.
  • Cal.com, for call bookings.
  • Google Analytics, for web analytics if the user accepts analytics cookies.
  • Chatbase, for the chatbot or automated assistant.
  • WhatsApp, for direct contact by messaging.
  • Email providers or technical infrastructure needed to answer communications.

Each provider may process data under its own terms and privacy policies. When the user interacts with external services, they should review the respective policies.

8. International data transfers

Some technology providers may be located outside the European Economic Area or use international infrastructure.

If international transfers take place, they will be covered by adequate safeguards under the GDPR, such as standard contractual clauses, adequacy decisions or other legally provided mechanisms.

Using services such as Google Analytics, Cal.com, Chatbase or WhatsApp may involve processing data outside the European Economic Area under those providers' terms.

9. User rights

Users can exercise the following data protection rights:

  • Access: to know what personal data is being processed.
  • Rectification: to request the correction of inaccurate data.
  • Erasure: to request the deletion of data where appropriate.
  • Objection: to object to certain processing.
  • Restriction of processing: to request that the use of the data be limited.
  • Portability: to receive the data in a structured format where applicable.
  • Withdrawal of consent: to withdraw the consent given at any time.

To exercise these rights, the user can write to:

info@tallerdedigitalizacion.com

The request must clearly state the right to be exercised, and additional information may be required to verify the identity of the applicant.

Users also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) if they consider that the processing of their data does not comply with the applicable rules.

The controller will apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no system connected to the Internet can guarantee absolute security. Users should use the website and the contact channels prudently and avoid sending sensitive or unnecessary information.

11. Website forms

The forms available on the website may ask for the data needed to answer enquiries, manage bookings, send materials, run self-assessments or keep in touch.

Fields marked as mandatory are required to handle the corresponding request. If the user does not provide that data, the request may not be handled.

By submitting a form, the user declares that the data provided is truthful, up to date and their own, or that they have sufficient authorisation to share it.

12. Operational Tax self-assessment

The website may include a self-assessment based on questions about operational areas of the company, such as management, processes, support, technical operation, digital identity, data, traceability, security and automation.

The answers entered may be used to calculate an indicative score, show weak areas, generate downloadable results and propose a follow-up conversation.

The self-assessment is not personalised professional advice and does not guarantee results. Its purpose is to give a first reading of the operational state as perceived by the user.

If the user makes contact through WhatsApp, their data will be processed to manage the conversation and answer the request.

Using WhatsApp means the user interacts with an external platform, subject to its own terms and privacy policy.

It is recommended not to send confidential, sensitive or unnecessary information over WhatsApp.

14. Bookings through Cal.com

If the user books a call through Cal.com, the data entered will be processed to manage the appointment, send confirmations, provide reminders and keep communication related to the meeting.

Cal.com acts as an external provider and may process data under its own terms and privacy policy.

The user should also review Cal.com's terms and privacy policy before using the tool.

The website incorporates Chatbase as a chatbot or automated assistant to answer frequently asked questions or provide information about the services.
